Editing on a phone that behaves like editing.

• Drag to move, drag the ends to trim, pinch to zoom
• Adjust opacity, scale, start, length and volume
• Colour: brightness, contrast, saturation, temperature
• Straighten a sideways clip, move it in the frame
• Waveforms on the timeline
• A clip's own sound goes into the export
• Steppers for precise cuts

Fixes: phone footage exports in the shape it was shot, exports carry the effects the editor shows, and a gap at the start no longer fails.
