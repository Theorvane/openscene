Editing on a phone that behaves like editing.

• Drag a clip to move it, drag its ends to trim, pinch to zoom the timeline
• Adjust panel for opacity, scale, start, length and volume — and what you set is what gets exported
• Colour on every clip: brightness, contrast, saturation and temperature
• Straighten a clip shot sideways, and move it around inside the frame
• Waveforms on the timeline, so audio is no longer edited blind
• A clip's own sound now goes into the export instead of being dropped
• Steppers for start and length, so a precise cut no longer depends on grabbing a 22-point edge

Fixes: footage shot on a phone exports in the shape it was shot, exported clips carry the effects the editor shows, and a timeline that starts with a gap exports instead of failing.
